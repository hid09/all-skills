# Empty setup example

When `WARDROBE_URL` and `WARDROBE_DATA_DIR` are both unavailable, do not invent owned items. Explain that the Skill is running without a private wardrobe source, then ask for either:

- a configured service or manifest; or
- a small manual list such as “navy wool coat, white shirt, black wide-leg trousers”.

For trend research, the user may also provide public links or screenshots. Treat those items as references only.
